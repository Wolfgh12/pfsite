The Foundations

What is project management, really?

Whether you are constructing a building, rolling out software, or organizing an event, every project comes down to four fundamental questions:

01

Scope (The Work)

Knowing exactly what needs to be delivered—and having the courage to say "no" to unplanned additions that drain time and money.

Goal: Prevent endless changes.
02

Schedule (The Time)

Breaking the work into clear steps, setting realistic deadlines, and spotting delays early so you can fix them before it is too late.

Goal: Deliver on the promised date.
03

Cost (The Money)

Knowing what every piece of work will cost, verifying contractor invoices, and avoiding embarrassing budget overruns.

Goal: Complete work within budget.
04

Risk (The Reality)

Anticipating what could go wrong—delayed shipments, bad weather, or staff shortages—and preparing backup plans before disaster strikes.

Goal: No unpleasant surprises.
How We Teach

No memorizing definitions. Only skills you use on the job.

Learn how to track spending against real progress. You will easily spot when a project is spending too fast and know how to explain the numbers to your leadership.

Avoid arguments with vendors and sub-contractors. Learn how to write clear expectations, manage change requests, and keep everyone accountable without lawsuits.
