Directorate Legacy & Philosophy

Turning project delivery from a gamble into an exacting science.

When Mike Kofi Kwatia Awuah founded Project Focus in 2001, high-value infrastructure projects across public and private sectors routinely fell victim to the same systemic failures: ballooning budgets, missed deadlines, and unmanaged contractual disputes.

Director Mike recognized that the industry was suffocating under academic models that failed the moment dirt met the ground. He established Project Focus on a singular premise: project management must be practiced as a live discipline of risk defense, schedule baseline control, and ruthless contractual governance.

Today, what started as an intensive elite workshop has expanded into an international powerhouse. Project Focus advises government ministries, multinationals, engineering joint ventures, and capital developers, maintaining the standard that Director Mike set twenty-five years ago.
